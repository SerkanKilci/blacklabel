What the app does — and doesn't do

Blacklabel scans packaged-food barcodes and shows a 0–100 score derived from publicly available ingredient, additive, and nutrition data (primarily from Open Food Facts, a community-maintained database we don't control and can't guarantee the accuracy of).

Not medical advice: this score is not medical advice, a diagnosis, or a treatment recommendation. Always check a product's official packaging and consult a qualified professional for health decisions.
